Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

BRANDON respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored in a hashed (scrambled) form and we cannot read them.
  • Delivery details: the names, phone numbers and addresses you save or enter at checkout.
  • Order details: the products you order, order amount, order status and payment method.
  • Payment information: for UPI payments we store the UPI transaction ID (UTR) you enter so we can verify your payment. For card or NetBanking payments, the payment partner handles your details and we do not see or store your card number.
  • Browser data: cookies and local storage on your device to keep you signed in and remember your cart, wishlist and preferences.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process, deliver and track your orders
  • To verify payments and prevent fraud
  • To handle cancellations, returns and refunds
  • To contact you about your orders or support requests
  • To improve the website and keep it secure

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what is needed with: courier and delivery partners (your name, phone number and address) and our payment partner when you pay by card or NetBanking. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and your browser's local storage to keep your cart, wishlist, sign-in session and basic settings. You can clear them from your browser settings, but parts of the website, such as your cart and staying signed in, may stop working.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including hashing passwords and limiting who can access order data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active and as needed for orders, returns, accounting and legal requirements.

Your choices

You can view and remove saved addresses from My Account. To correct your details or to ask us to delete your account and personal data, contact us using the details below. Some order records may need to be kept as required by law.

Children

Our store is meant for adults. Children's clothing is sold to parents and guardians, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

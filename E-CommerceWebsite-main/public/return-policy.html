Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

We want you to be happy with what you order from BRANDON. If something is not right, you can cancel your order or request a return as explained below.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order yourself from My Orders using the Cancel Order button while its status is Placed or Confirmed. Once an order is packed or shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery as per this policy. If you already paid online, the amount is refunded as described under Refunds below.

Return window

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery. Go to My Orders, open the delivered order and choose Request Return with a short reason. Our team will review the request and reply with the next steps.

What can be returned

  • Wrong item received
  • Damaged or defective item
  • Item not as described on the product page
  • Size or fit issue

To be accepted, the item must be unused, unwashed and unaltered, with all tags and original packaging intact.

What cannot be returned

  • Items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery
  • Items returned without original tags or packaging
  • Innerwear, socks and other hygiene-related items
  • Products marked as non-returnable on the product page
  • Return requests made after the 7-day window

Damaged or wrong item

If your parcel arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos of the item and the packaging, along with your Order ID. We will arrange a replacement or a refund.

Refunds

  • A refund is started after the returned item reaches us and passes a quality check.
  • Approved refunds are sent within 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method (the UPI ID or bank account used to pay).
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, the refund is sent by UPI or bank transfer. We will ask you for your UPI ID or bank details.
  • If your online payment failed or was rejected during verification, the order is cancelled and any amount debited is returned to you.

The time your bank takes to show the money may be a little longer than the time we take to send it.

Sending the item back

When your return request is approved, we will tell you how and where to send the item back. Please pack it securely in its original packaging.
